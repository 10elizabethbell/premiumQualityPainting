# Product: Premium Quality Painting website

- **Platform:** web
- **Stack:** static single-file HTML/CSS/JS (`index.html`), images in `img/`, hosted on GitHub Pages. Only external resource: Cal Sans from Google Fonts.
- **Source:** Muse brief, 2026-10-20 (one cabinet-painting ad cross-posted to three NJ contractor Facebook groups).

## Users
- Homeowners in Monmouth County, NJ and nearby who want their cabinets painted instead of replaced. They mostly arrive on a phone, from a Facebook link, inside Facebook's in-app browser.
- The owner, who will receive the site for free and judge it in one phone screen. The ad was posted by Fernando Oliveira's personal profile; whether he is the owner is **unconfirmed**, so the site names nobody.

## Purpose
Turn a visitor into a quote request: a pre-written text message to (848) 667-3925, or a call.

## Positioning
A cabinet-painting specialist, not a general painter. Their words (ad caption, 2026-10-20): "Give your kitchen a stunning new look with professional cabinet painting!", "Detailed preparation", "Smooth, beautiful finish", "A fresh look for the kitchen you love", "Serving Monmouth County, NJ, and surrounding areas".

## Capabilities and constraints
- Contact: phone (848) 667-3925, accepts texts ("Call or text"). No email, no booking link, no business Facebook page.
- Facebook: only the poster's personal profile (https://facebook.com/profile.php?id=61555626455608, not API-readable). Used for "see more work" because it's where their work is posted.
- No reviews, prices, hours, years in business, insurance, licence or address on record; the site says nothing about them.
- Phone-first; no sticky bottom bar; phone number only top-right on the first screen.

## Brand commitments
- Logo: gold serif capitals "PREMIUM QUALITY PAINTING" on black (from the ad's flyer). The site uses that lettering, re-coloured navy on paper and kept gold on navy.
- Gold accent (#e4b95c, sampled from the flyer). Navy ground (**inferred**: the flyer is black, and the house rule is no black grounds; navy walls appear in their own job photos and "bold navy" in their caption).
- Voice: upbeat, plain, "we / us" (the ad says "Send us a message and let's talk!"). Hook from their caption: "What color would you choose for your kitchen cabinets?"

## Evidence on hand
- One flyer with two before/after pairs of the same room: a study/library with wood built-ins, shelves and a desk, painted deep green with brass pulls. About 290px per panel. The two angles in each pair don't line up, so they are shown as pairs, not a slider.
- **Not a kitchen.** The ad is about kitchens, the photos show built-ins; the page says "cabinets and built-ins" and never calls the photos a kitchen.

## Principles
1. The owner sees their own business, done better than they imagined, in one phone screen.
2. Only facts from the brief; inferences are marked in HANDOFF.md.
3. The quote request is the one action; everything else supports it.
4. The transformation (wood to colour) is the story.
